Game of Thrones

IF YOU WANT TO PLAY YOU NEED TO REVERT TO HOI4 1.16.10 OR THE MOD WON'T WORK!!!

Introduction

Welcome to Game of Thrones, a complete overhaul of Hearts of Iron IV, bringing the epic world of Westeros to life. Immerse yourself in the brutal politics, legendary battles, and complex alliances of the Seven Kingdoms. Dive into an experience where knights ride to war, dragons soar across the skies, and the fate of Westeros hangs in the balance.

Description

A Complete Medieval Overhaul:
Game of Thrones transforms Hearts of Iron IV into a medieval strategy experience, replacing tanks with knights, planes with dragons, and ironclads with wooden ships. Play through the seasons of Game of Thrones, and guide your favorite factions to victory. Everything from units to techs, doctrines, resources, and special projects is reskinned to reflect the gritty medieval world of Westeros.

Rich Lore and Dynamic Events:
From the political intrigue in King's Landing to the icy frontier of the North, Game of Thrones delivers a rich narrative with events based on major moments from the TV Series. Will you secure the Iron Throne for House Stark or House Lannister? Will the Ironborn plunder the shores of the North? Every decision you make will shape the future of Westeros.

Enhanced Visuals and Audio:
Everything in Game of Thrones has been reskinned to create a truly medieval atmosphere. From the warhorses on the battlefield to the castles of Westeros, the visual overhaul brings the world of Game of Thrones to life like never before. For an added challenge, you can choose to limit the dragons by selecting GOT Mode at the beginning of the game.

Features Include:
- Complete reskin of units, techs, and doctrines to fit the medieval world of Westeros.
- Events covering Seasons 1-4, with key moments from the Game of Thrones series. (All 8 Seasons are planned)
