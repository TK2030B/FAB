Game of Thrones Legacy: A Game of Thrones Mod

[img]https://i.imgur.com/NVBHkZ6.png[/img]

Welcome to Game of Thrones Legacy, a complete overhaul of Hearts of Iron IV, bringing the epic world of Westeros to life. Immerse yourself in the brutal politics, legendary battles, and complex alliances of the Seven Kingdoms. Dive into an experience where knights ride to war, dragons soar across the skies, and the fate of Westeros hangs in the balance.

[img]https://i.imgur.com/6OXWwEl.png[/img]

A Complete Medieval Overhaul:
Game of Thrones Legacy transforms Hearts of Iron IV into a medieval strategy experience, replacing tanks with knights, planes with dragons, and ironclads with wooden ships. Play through the early seasons of Game of Thrones, with events covering Seasons 1 & 2, and guide your favorite factions to victory. Everything from units to techs, doctrines, resources, and special projects is reskinned to reflect the gritty medieval world of Westeros.

Rich Lore and Dynamic Events:
From the political intrigue in King's Landing to the icy frontier of the North, Game of Thrones Legacy delivers a rich narrative with events based on major moments from the first two seasons. Will you secure the Iron Throne for House Stark or House Lannister? Will the Ironborn plunder the shores of the North? Every decision you make will shape the future of Westeros.

Play Your Way:
Historical Mode: For purists, the game can be played with historical AI focuses, where factions will stay true to the events of the series. Experience the war for the Iron Throne as it was meant to be — with an emphasis on strategy and historical accuracy.

Sandbox Mode: Want to create your own tale of power and betrayal? Turn on sandbox mode and unleash chaos. You’ll have complete freedom to shape Westeros however you wish, whether it’s setting up alliances, waging unconventional wars, or rewriting history entirely.

Enhanced Visuals and Audio:
Everything in Game of Thrones Legacy has been reskinned to create a truly medieval atmosphere. From the warhorses on the battlefield to the castles of Westeros, the visual overhaul brings the world of Game of Thrones to life like never before. For an added challenge, you can choose to limit the dragons by selecting GOT Mode at the beginning of the game.

For Fans of Game of Thrones:
This mod is designed specifically for fans of the Game of Thrones series who want to see the world of Westeros in the form of Hearts of Iron IV. Whether you want to fight for the Iron Throne or simply witness the rise and fall of your favorite houses, Game of Thrones Legacy offers a deep and immersive experience that stays true to the lore.

Features Include:
- Complete reskin of units, techs, and doctrines to fit the medieval world of Westeros.
- Events covering Seasons 1 & 2, with key moments from the Game of Thrones series. (All 8 Seasons are planned)
- Two modes of play: Historical Mode for lore-based gameplay, and Sandbox Mode for freeform, chaotic fun.
- GOT Mode to limit dragons for a more grounded medieval experience.
